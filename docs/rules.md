# KNHB Under 10 (O10) Match Rules & Guidelines

These rules are based on the Official Youth Hockey Guide (Standard 8v8 Competition Format) and outline the core philosophy, pitch layout, and key match rules.

## Core Philosophy: The Game Leader
In Under 10 youth hockey, matches are led by **Game Leaders (Speelbegeleiders)** rather than formal referees. The goal is to facilitate a fun, safe, active, and educational learning environment for young players.

1. **Enjoyment:** Keep the game flowing, encourage all children, and ensure every child has an enjoyable match experience.
2. **Safety:** Prevent danger immediately (high balls, wild swings, hard contact) and step in when play becomes hazardous.
3. **Learning:** Briefly explain rules when calling infractions so players understand why play was stopped.

## Field Setup & Team Organization
- **Pitch Dimensions:** Played across one half of a standard hockey pitch (~55 M × 43 M).
- **Goals:** Goals are placed at the standard sideline / backline with standard scoring circles marked.
- **Match Duration:** 2 × 30 minutes, with a 5-minute half-time break.
- **Team Size:** 8 vs 8 (7 field players and 1 fully equipped goalkeeper).
- **Substitutions:** Rolling substitutions are permitted at any time during play (except during penalty corner/shootout setup).

## Core Match Rules

### 1. Start of Play (Beginslag)
- Taken from the center mark at the start of each half and after every conceded goal.
- The ball can be pushed, hit, or self-passed in any direction.
- Opponents must maintain a minimum distance of **5 meters**.

### 2. Free Hits & Restarts (Spelhervattingen)
- Awarded for standard rule infractions occurring outside the shooting circle.
- **Self-pass:** Players can quickly restart play by taking a self-pass (moving the ball at least 1 meter before playing it again).
- All defending players must remain at least **5 meters away** from the restart location.

### 3. Scoring a Goal (Doelpunt)
- A goal is valid only when an attacking player touches the ball inside the shooting circle before it crosses the goal line.
- The ball must enter the goal at or below backboard height.

### 4. Safety & Fair Play (Gevaarlijk Spel)
- **High Balls:** Raising the ball dangerously into opposing players is strictly prohibited.
- **Stick Handling:** Sticks must not be swung dangerously above shoulder height near other players.
- **Body Contact:** No physical pushing, body checks, or deliberate physical obstruction.

### 5. Infractions Inside the Scoring Circle
- Minor defensive fouls or unintentional offenses result in a restart outside the circle (5 meters from circle edge) or a designated youth shootout setup.

## Game Leader Timeline & Responsibilities

**Before the Match:**
- Coordinate with the opposing game leader and align on tone and positioning.
- Check equipment, pitch safety, and goal placement.
- Greet players warmly and remind them to play fairly.

**During the Match:**
- **Whistle with Purpose:** Use the whistle to clarify decisions, prioritizing game flow over minor technicalities.
- **Keep the Flow:** Encourage quick restarts (self-pass) and enforce the 5 meters distance rule.
- **Explain & Guide:** Briefly explain fouls to players (e.g., "Watch out, rounded side of the stick!").
- **Manage Sidelines:** Ensure coaches guide supportively and parents cheer positively behind barriers.

**After the Match:**
- Facilitate the post-match handshake / stick-tap between both teams.
- Congratulate both sides and finalize administrative sign-offs (DWF) with team managers.
