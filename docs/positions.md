# Tactical Formations & Positions

## Formations

The default formation is the **1-2-3-2** configuration, suited for an attacking, wide-play style on the cross-pitch format.

### 1-2-3-2 Positions (8 players)
1. **GK (Goalkeeper)**
2. **LB (Left Back)**
3. **RB (Right Back)**
4. **LM (Left Midfield)**
5. **CM (Center Midfield)**
6. **RM (Right Midfield)**
7. **LS (Left Striker)**
8. **RS (Right Striker)**

*Note: There are also two substitutes at any given time, designated as **SUB-DEF** and **SUB-MID** to manage rotations.*

### Alternative Formations
* **Defensive (1-3-3-1)**
* **Possession (1-2-4-1)**
* **Attack (1-1-3-3)**
